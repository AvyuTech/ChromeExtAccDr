# Nearhood: Phase 1 scope

Nearhood is for friends, family and neighbours to recommend good things to each other and do things together. It is not a booking, payments or deals app. Phase 1 stays small, human and curated; anything that needs money, contracts or inventory waits for Phase 2.

## What Phase 1 is

| Pillar | What people do |
| --- | --- |
| **Friends** | Follow friends and family, see what they ate, did and recommend, keep a *Want to try* list, plan together. |
| **Places** | Check in with one tap (Must try / Good / Skip). Places are added and confirmed by people. |
| **Clubs** | Neighbours, founders, cricket lovers, parents, runners, book lovers start clubs, meet up and buy things together. |
| **City** | A light layer that helps everyone: weather, air, a traffic heat map with the best route, verified alerts, reporting civic issues, safety. |
| **Ask** | One question, one answer merged from friends, neighbours and labelled public sources: *Nearhood says*. |

## Who creates what

| Thing | Who creates it | How it stays honest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Places** | Any verified resident, the first time they check in somewhere new. | Listed after 3 verified visits or a guide's check. Owners can claim a page only to fix hours, menu and photos, and reply. No business listings, no ads, no paid ranking. |
| **New openings** | Locals mark them as *Spotted*. | Neighbours confirm by visiting; nothing waits for us or for an AI. |
| **Clubs** | Any verified resident. | Club rules and admins are visible; members are real people. |
| **Meetups** | Club members, inside their club (Sunday cricket, founders' breakfast). | Just who's in and where. No tickets, no payments. |
| **Group buys** | Club members ("buying an SUV, 3+ cars gets a fleet discount"). | Nearhood only gathers people; the deal and the money happen outside the app. |
| **City events** | Not created in the app. They come from public sources (ticket sites, organisers' pages, reels, news). | Labelled with their source, link out for details and tickets. Friends' "want to go" is the social signal. |
| **Alerts** | Official feeds (police, AMC, utilities, IMD) and verified neighbours. | Marked verified or unconfirmed. |

## Knowing who people are

Trust comes from knowing who is talking. Every member's profile shows:

- **What they do**: their work in their own words (paediatrician, architect, founder), with a tick when confirmed by a work email, a professional licence or LinkedIn. It also shows next to their posts and replies, so an answer from a doctor reads as one.
- **One-time checks**: phone (one-time code), government ID (matched once through DigiLocker; Nearhood keeps the result, never the number), home neighbourhood (utility bill or two neighbours) and how many neighbours vouched for them.
- **Ask me about**: a few topics they're happy to help with.

Only verified residents can post, review and run clubs.

## Filling the city before people do

A new city is empty on day one. Public sources fill the gap so the app is useful immediately:

- Reels and posts about places and dishes, news, organisers' event pages and official feeds become **labelled source content** ("Seen on 12 reels", "From the organiser's Instagram").
- Source content never appears as a person's review. It can rank and describe, but verdicts only come from people with verified visits.
- As friends and neighbours check in, their verdicts take over and source content steps back.

## Other cities

Kept simple: when friends check in on a trip, it shows in your friends feed tagged with the city ("In Mumbai") and stays out of your home city's lists. No city switching in Phase 1.

## Out of Phase 1 (cut)

Bill payments and city services, deals and coupons, booking or ticketing, RSVPs with counts for city events, movie listings, public transport (bus, BRTS, metro), any payment flow, business accounts that post.

## Phase 2 (later, only if Phase 1 works)

Collecting money for group buys through a partner, claimed pages with richer owner tools, verified club events with capped sign-ups, browsing another city through friends' check-ins.
