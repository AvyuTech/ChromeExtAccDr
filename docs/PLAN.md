# Nearhood: Phase 1 scope

Nearhood is for friends, family and neighbours to recommend good things to each other and do things together. It is not a booking, payments or deals app. Phase 1 stays small, human and curated; anything that needs money, contracts or inventory waits for Phase 2.

## What Phase 1 is

| Pillar | What people do |
| --- | --- |
| **Friends** | Follow friends and family, see what they ate, did and recommend, keep a *Want to try* list, plan together. |
| **Places** | Check in with one tap (Must try / Good / Skip). Places are added and confirmed by people. |
| **Clubs** | Neighbours, founders, cricket lovers, parents, runners, book lovers start clubs, meet up and buy things together. |
| **City** | A light layer that helps everyone: weather, air, a traffic heat map with the best route, verified alerts, reporting civic issues, safety. |
| **Ask** | One question, one answer merged from friends, neighbours and labelled public sources: *Nearhood says*. |

## Only three things: profiles, pages and clubs

Everything in Nearhood belongs to one of three things. There is nothing else to learn.

| Thing | What it is | Who runs it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Profile** | A real, verified person. | That person. |
| **Page** | Any place worth knowing: a café, a pickleball court, a lake, a shop, a temple, a day-trip spot. | Started by the local who added it, kept right by locals and guides; the owner can claim it. |
| **Club** | People who do something together: neighbours, founders, cricket, parents, book lovers. | The members who started it, as admins. |

Everything else is something one of them posts: posts and questions, check-ins, events, meetups and group buys. An event is a post by a person ("Riya shared an event"), a page ("Pickle & Play · beginners' social") or a club ("Thaltej Parents · kite workshop"), and it links out for details or tickets. A meetup or group buy is a post in a club and always shows which club it's from.

## Telling them apart at a glance

Pages and clubs work in similar ways (both have people who run them, both can post events), so they must *look* different everywhere they appear: headers, lists, search, feed tags and event hosts.

| | Profile | Business page | Public place | Club |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shape | Round | Square | Square | Square with a double ring |
| Mark | none | Shop front | Tree | People |
| Label | name | *Business page* | *Public place* | *Club* |
| Main action | Follow | Check in | Check in | Join |
| You… | follow a person | visit and save it | visit and save it | join it |
| Who posts | the person | only the people who run it: updates and events, two a week | nobody runs it; locals and guides keep it right | every member; admins pin and remove |
| Who runs it | themselves | the owner who claimed it (plus staff later) | locals and guides | the admins who started it |
| Events | can share one | posts its own | locals share them | members plan meetups and group buys |

The rule of thumb: **a page is a place you go to; a club is people you join.**

## Pages: one template for everything

Every page looks and works the same, whatever it is. No restaurant pages, sports pages or park pages with their own layouts. The order never changes; a section only hides when it has nothing in it:

1. Cover, photo, *must try* %, locals, distance; name, what it is, area, price, open now.
2. Who added it and how many locals confirmed it; who runs it (or *Claim it*, or *Public place*).
3. One main button, **Check in** (*Manage page* if you run it), then three small icons: **Save** (saving is the same as *Want to try*), **Directions** and **Share** (send to friends, copy the link or another app; this replaced a separate Recommend). *Ask about it* sits on *What locals say*.
4. Community verdict (Must try / Good / Skip from verified visits).
5. Friends who've been.
6. Most loved here: the dishes, sessions or things people pick most, with photos.
7. What locals say (merged summary, sources labelled) and good to know.
8. Hours and address.
9. From the page: the owner's updates and events, only once claimed.
10. Reviews from people you trust; talk about it; similar pages.

Check-ins are one flow for every page: pick the page, give a verdict, say what stood out, add a note or photo.

## How a page is made

- **Who:** any member whose phone and home neighbourhood are verified. Businesses can't create promotional listings; an owner adds their place like anyone else and then claims it.
- **Where:** the **+** button ▸ *Add a page*; searching Explore with no result ▸ *Can't find it? Add a page*; Explore ▸ *Spotted* ▸ *Spotted something new?*; checking in ▸ *Not listed? Add a page*.
- **Steps:** search first (so there are no duplicates) ▸ name, what it is, neighbourhood ▸ your own photo ▸ one line on why it's worth knowing and your connection to it (just found it, I work there, I run it).
- **Going live:** it starts as *Spotted* (0 of 3). It goes live when three verified locals check in or confirm it, or one Nearhood guide confirms it. The person who added it keeps *Added by* credit for good and earns helpful points when it goes live.
- **Keeping it right:** locals suggest edits, guides merge duplicates and mark places closed after three reports. Public places (lakes, parks, temples) are never claimable; locals and guides keep them up to date.

## Claiming a page

Claiming exists so owners can keep facts right, not to buy attention.

1. **Start:** on the page, *Run this place? Claim it* (or the page's ⋮).
2. **Your role:** owner, partner or manager.
3. **You:** your Nearhood ID must be verified (DigiLocker, once).
4. **Proof in the page's name:** one of GST certificate, FSSAI licence, Shop & Establishment (Gumasta) registration or Udyam certificate.
5. **Reach the venue:** we call the phone number locals listed for the page with a 6-digit code; you enter it, then send the claim.
6. **Review:** the Nearhood team checks the documents within 2 working days; if anything is unclear, a local guide visits. The page shows *Claim pending · you* with a timeline: sent ▸ venue phone confirmed ▸ documents checked ▸ approved.
7. **Approved:** the page says *Run by Name, owner*.

**Running a page** (Manage page, from the page or Me ▸ Pages you run):

- Edit hours, phone, address, what's offered and the cover photo. Each change shows on the page as *Updated by the owner* and goes into the page's **public history**, which nobody can delete from. If locals say a fact is wrong, a guide checks it.
- Post up to 2 updates a week (an optional link out for booking or details). They show on the page and reach people who saved it; never the Home feed, never ads.
- Reply once, in public, to any check-in, marked *Owner · Page name*. No hiding, editing or deleting check-ins; *Report to guides* is the only option, and guides decide.
- See simple weekly counts (saves, check-ins, must-try %), never who.

**What keeps owners honest:** an owner's own check-ins, verdicts and votes on their page never count (the Check in button becomes Manage); everything they post anywhere carries a *Runs Page name* tag, so a recommendation from an owner always reads as one; every change is in the public history; and owners can never pay for ranking or placement, see who saved them, or message people who didn't write first.

In the prototype, Jay Mehta runs **Brew Lab** (a café) and **Pickle & Play** (pickleball courts), to show the same owner tools on two very different pages; Seoul Bowl, Kettle & Crumb, Taco Tiempo and Board & Brew are unclaimed for trying the claim flow. If a business changes hands, the new owner claims it with fresh proof and the old owner is told. A second claim on the same page gets both sides asked for proof.

## Who creates what

| Thing | Who creates it | How it stays honest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pages** | Any verified resident (see *How a page is made*). | Live after 3 verified locals or a guide confirm it. Owners can claim a page to keep facts right and post updates on it. No ads, no paid ranking. |
| **New openings** | Locals mark them as *Spotted*. | Neighbours confirm by visiting; nothing waits for us or for an AI. |
| **Clubs** | Any verified resident. | Club rules and admins are visible; members are real people. |
| **Meetups** | Club members, inside their club (Sunday cricket, founders' breakfast). | Just who's in and where. No tickets, no payments. |
| **Group buys** | Club members ("buying an SUV, 3+ cars gets a fleet discount"). | Nearhood only gathers people; the deal and the money happen outside the app. |
| **Events** | Posted by a person, a page (the venue) or a club; many are shared from public sources (ticket sites, organisers' pages, reels, news). | Labelled with who posted them and where they were seen; link out for details and tickets. Friends' "want to go" is the social signal. |
| **Alerts** | Official feeds (police, AMC, utilities, IMD) and verified neighbours. | Marked verified or unconfirmed. |

## Knowing who people are

Trust comes from knowing who is talking. Every member's profile shows:

- **What they do**: their work in their own words (paediatrician, architect, founder), with a tick when confirmed by a work email, a professional licence or LinkedIn. It also shows next to their posts and replies, so an answer from a doctor reads as one.
- **One-time checks**: phone (one-time code), government ID (matched once through DigiLocker; Nearhood keeps the result, never the number), home neighbourhood (utility bill or two neighbours) and how many neighbours vouched for them.
- **Ask me about**: a few topics they're happy to help with.

Only verified residents can post, review and run clubs.

## Borrowed from Reddit: votes, threads, sorting

Reddit's best ideas keep good answers on top without anyone in charge. Nearhood uses them, with real names:

- **Up and down votes, and nothing else:** every post, reply, check-in, meetup, group buy and city report uses the same ▲ score ▼. No likes or hearts anywhere, and no separate +1 on city issues (voting a report up means it affects you too). One vote each; tap again to take it back. Only verified residents' votes count.
- **Down votes sink, they never punish:** they don't take helpful points away. A reply at −4 or lower starts *folded* ("folded by neighbours' votes") but is never deleted, so anyone can open it.
- **Threads:** reply to a reply; conversations nest under the answer they're about. Tap the line beside a thread to fold it. The person who posted is tagged *Author*.
- **Sorting replies:** *Most helpful* (marked helpful, then people known for helping, then votes), *Top* (votes) and *New*.
- **Sorting a club:** *Hot* (votes that fade with age), *New* and *Top*. Admins can pin a note (house rules, water timings) to the top of their club; pinned notes stay in the club and never enter the Home feed.
- **Not borrowed:** anonymous accounts, paid awards, karma for posting volume, and subreddit-style mods who can delete anything. Admins can pin and remove rule-breaking posts; votes do the rest.

## Helpful points, levels and City heroes

Nearhood rewards being useful to other people, not posting a lot. Points only come from what *other verified people* do with your contribution; posting, checking in or commenting earns nothing on its own.

**Earning helpful points**

| When | Points |
| --- | --- |
| The person who asked marks your reply *Helpful* | +15 |
| A verified resident upvotes your post or reply | +2 (at most 20 a day from upvotes) |
| Someone taps *This helped me* or saves to *Want to try* from your check-in | +5 |
| A page you added goes live | +25 |
| You were one of the first three to confirm a new opening | +5 |
| A city issue you reported is confirmed fixed | +20 |
| Lost & found reunited through your post or reply | +30 |
| You hosted a meetup and 5+ people came | +20 |
| Your group buy reached its target | +15 |
| Someone you vouched for got verified | +5 |
| Your post was removed after reports | −50 |
| A check-in of yours was confirmed fake | −100, level frozen for 30 days |

**Fair play:** down votes never take points away (they only sink and fold); at most 100 points a day; the same person's votes count once a week; unverified accounts' votes don't count; pairs who keep voting for each other are zeroed; points can never be bought.

**Levels** (lifetime points plus trust checks; a level waits until the check is done)

| Level | Points | Also needs | Unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Newcomer | 0 | | Ask, reply, check in, join clubs |
| Neighbour | 50 | Phone | Add pages, plan meetups |
| Regular | 200 | Home neighbourhood verified | Start clubs and group buys |
| Local expert | 600 | ID verified | *Local expert in Food* on answers in that topic; shown first in replies and in *Nearhood says* |
| Nearhood guide | 1,500 | 10 vouches | One-tap confirm new pages, merge duplicates, handle reports, second review on claims |
| City hero | 4,000 | 25 vouches, active in the last 90 days | City hero badge, the City heroes board, the quarterly city heroes meetup, shaping city guides |

Points are tagged by topic (Food, Cafés, Traffic & roads, Parenting, Pets, Fitness & sports, Lost & found, Civic issues, New openings, Weekend plans), so profiles show what someone is *known for*.

**Rankings, many ways:** the City heroes board ranks verified residents by scope (my neighbourhood, the city, my clubs, friends), period (this month or all time) and measure (helpful points, helpful answers, pages added, issues fixed, meetups hosted, rising this month), filtered by topic. Seasons reset on the 1st; levels never reset. People can hide themselves from boards in Settings ▸ Privacy.

**Where it shows:** helpful points are the second headline number on every profile, next to followers (so fame and contribution sit side by side); the level is a pill beside the name; a quiet level tag sits next to names on posts and replies (only from Local expert up, so feeds stay calm); replies order by *Most helpful*; *Most helpful here* on every club and *Most helpful this month* on Home; the City heroes board; and Me ▸ *Your impact* (progress, what's missing, points history).

**Explained in the app:** an ⓘ beside every helpful number (profiles, City heroes, Your impact, Home and clubs) opens the six-step loop in a sheet, and **Settings ▸ Helpful points & levels** has the whole plan: the loop, how you earn, levels, rankings, where it shows and fair play.

**The loop, end to end:** you help → neighbours say it helped → you earn points (only from verified people, capped) → your level rises (with identity checks) → you stand out (points beside followers, level on your answers, rank on City heroes) → *help comes back*: your own questions, reports and plans reach more local experts and neighbours. The last step is marked *Coming next* in the app; it isn't built yet.

## Filling the city before people do

A new city is empty on day one. Phase 1 fills it with people, not scraping, because events, reels and listings are hard and risky to collect:

- **Founding guides:** before launch, around 50 verified locals per city (food writers, sports club captains, heritage walkers, parents' group admins) add and check the first few hundred pages and lists. They start as Nearhood guides.
- **Links people paste:** a reel, an organiser's page or a news story can be attached to a check-in, page or event by a person, and shows as a labelled link. Nothing is pulled in automatically.
- **Official feeds only for city info:** alerts and water timings come from the city and utilities, labelled.
- Verdicts only ever come from people with verified visits.

## Explore: finding pages

Explore is for finding pages, and everything on it is something people made: pages they added and confirmed, their check-ins and their lists. One row of filters (Cafés, Food, Sports, Outdoors, Culture, Shopping, Day trips) applies to everything below it.

**The map leads.** It sits right under the filters, big, and opens full screen (*Full map*, or the expand button):

- **Roam the city:** drag to move (with a little glide), pinch, scroll or double-tap to zoom, + and − buttons, and a button to come back to where you live. The city goes well past your neighbourhood, from Gota to Maninagar and Bopal to the old city.
- **Choose what to see:** *Businesses*, *Public places* and *Clubs* at the top, on or off, with counts. The category filters narrow businesses and public places.
- **Shape says what it is, colour says how good it is:** squares are business pages, circles public places, double-ringed squares clubs; dashed squares are new places still being confirmed. Green is loved (70%+ must try), violet liked (50–69%), amber mixed, grey new; clubs are blue when they have meetups coming up. A friend's face on a pin means they've been. *Colours* explains it on the map.
- **Zoom in for more:** zoomed out, pins are small dots; zoom in and they show their score (59%, 2.4k members); further in, their names.
- **Tap a pin** for a small card: photo, what it is, the score and locals, open now, friends who've been (for a club: members and the next meetup; for a spotted place: *I've been*). **Tap the card** to open the page.
- **Swipe the card** left for the next nearest spot and right to go back ("2 of 23 nearby"); the map follows each one. Arrows and the keyboard do the same. It walks outward from the first spot you tapped, across everything that's switched on: businesses, public places and clubs.

Below the map: friends' must-tries, new in the city, popular this week and lists by locals. There is no events or clubs section in the list: clubs are on the map and have their own tab, and events are posts by a person, a page or a club.

## Profiles: the short version

Followers are the number people care about, so it is the big number next to the photo; following and check-ins sit small beneath it. Then the name with a tick when fully verified and a small level pill, one line for work and area, *Runs …* if they run pages, and a two-line bio. One main action (Follow, or Edit profile on your own) and small icons (Message, Share, More). Tabs: Check-ins, Want to try, Lists. Everything else (verification details, helpful points and rank, known for, ask me about, interests, taste match, clubs, member since) sits in **About**.

Because followers matter, only verified people count as followers, and followers can't be bought.

## Other cities

Kept simple: when friends check in on a trip, it shows in your friends feed tagged with the city ("In Mumbai") and stays out of your home city's lists. No city switching in Phase 1.

## Out of Phase 1 (cut)

Bill payments and city services, deals and coupons, booking or ticketing, RSVPs with counts for city events, movie listings, public transport (bus, BRTS, metro), any payment flow, ads or business posts in the Home feed (a claimed page's updates stay on its page and reach people who saved it).

## Phase 2 (later, only if Phase 1 works)

Collecting money for group buys through a partner, richer owner tools for claimed pages, verified club events with capped sign-ups, browsing another city through friends' check-ins.
